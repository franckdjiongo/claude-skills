# SEO Marketing Action Plan Reference

Post-implementation manual for business owners. Generate this as part of the SEO_IMPROVEMENTS.md documentation.

## Phase Structure

### Phase 1: Foundation Setup (Week 1-2)

1. **Google Business Profile** — Claim/verify at business.google.com
   - Primary + secondary categories (adapt to business type)
   - Complete all fields, 10+ photos, review link + QR code
   - Post every 2 weeks minimum (inactivity penalty after 30 days)

2. **Google Search Console** — Domain-type property, DNS TXT verification
   - Submit sitemap, set weekly review cadence
   - Monitor: Performance, Indexing, Core Web Vitals, Enhancements, Security

3. **Bing Webmaster Tools** — Import from GSC or manual setup
   - Submit sitemap, implement IndexNow
   - Monitor AI Citations Performance Data monthly

4. **GA4 + GSC Integration** — Link via Admin > Product Links
   - See queries + post-landing behavior together

5. **Validate Structured Data** — Google Rich Results Test + Schema.org Validator

### Phase 2: Directory & Citation Building (Week 2-4)

**Tier 1 directories** (highest DR, submit immediately):
- Clutch.co (DR 91), G2.com (DR 92), Capterra (DR 91), DesignRush (DR 75), GoodFirms (DR 78)

**Tier 2** (within 2 weeks):
- BuiltIn, TrustRadius, PeerSpot, SoftwareAdvice, SourceForge
- Industry-specific directories (adapt to business vertical)

**Tier 3** (within first month):
- Crunchbase, Wellfound, ProductHunt (for SaaS), BetaList, The Manifest, TopDevelopers

**Local citations** (NAP consistency is critical):
- Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook, LinkedIn
- Local directories, Yellow Pages, Chamber of Commerce, industry associations
- Data aggregators: Neustar Localeze, Express Update
- Target: 30-50 consistent citations

### Phase 3: Content & Authority Building (Ongoing)

**Pillar-Cluster Content Model**:
- 4-5 pillar pages (2,500-4,000 words each)
- 5-10 cluster posts per pillar (800-1,500 words)
- Internal linking: clusters → pillar, pillar → clusters, cross-cluster
- 3-5 internal links per 1,000 words
- Publish 2-4 posts/month

**Link Building** (ranked by effectiveness):
1. Original research & data (156% more links vs. generic content)
2. Digital PR / Newsjacking (pitch within 4-24 hours)
3. Guest thought leadership (1-2/month on DA 40+ sites)
4. Expert source platforms (Connectively, Qwoted, SourceBottle)
5. Podcast guesting (1-3 backlinks per appearance)
6. Competitor backlink gap analysis
7. Resource page outreach
8. Event sponsorship

**Review Generation**:
- Target platforms: GBP, Clutch, G2
- Two-step ask: satisfaction check → review request
- Follow up at 48 hours and 1 week
- Respond to every review within 24-48 hours
- Target: 1 detailed review/month minimum

### Phase 4: Social Signals (Ongoing)

**LinkedIn** (primary B2B, 80% of B2B social leads):
- 3-5 posts/week, 5-10 thoughtful comments/day
- Carousels and micro-essays outperform other formats
- Share blog posts with native text summaries

**Other**: Twitter/X (industry commentary), Reddit (genuine participation), YouTube, Facebook (NAP)

### Phase 5: Video & Audio SEO (Month 2-3)

**YouTube**:
- Short-form (60-90s) for discovery, long-form (5-15 min) for authority
- Keyword-rich titles, 200+ word descriptions, timestamps, full transcripts
- Embed on corresponding website pages with VideoObject schema

**Podcast**:
- Record video-first, extract audio
- Publish transcript as blog post per episode
- Repurposing chain: video → audio → blog → clips → quotes → newsletter

### Phase 6: Email Marketing (Ongoing)

- Newsletter signup CTAs on pillar pages and homepage
- Lead magnet for signup (checklist, calculator, guide)
- Weekly/biweekly newsletter driving traffic to 2-3 pages
- Segment: prospects vs. clients vs. partners
- ROI: $36 per $1 spent, 40x more conversions than social

### Phase 7: PR & Media (Month 2+)

- Expert source platforms: Connectively, Qwoted, SourceBottle, Featured.com
- Media kit: boilerplate, bio, headshots, stats, expertise areas
- Newsjacking: Google Alerts → pitch within 4 hours
- Original research 1-2x/year
- Local PR: business publications, chamber newsletters

### Phase 8: Advanced Technical (Month 3-6)

- Prerendering/SSG for SPA SEO
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1
- Image optimization (WebP/AVIF), lazy loading, font subsetting

## Monthly Maintenance Checklist

**Week 1**: GSC/GA4 performance review, Bing AI Citations, CTR optimization
**Week 2**: Technical health (indexing errors, broken links, CWV, sitemap)
**Week 3**: Content publishing, GBP update, newsletter, reviews, LinkedIn
**Week 4**: Competitor check, NAP audit, content planning, backlink review

**Quarterly**: Full competitor analysis, content audit, citation audit, schema validation
**Annually**: Full SEO audit, keyword strategy refresh, security.txt renewal
