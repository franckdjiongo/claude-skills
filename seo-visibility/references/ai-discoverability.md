# AI Discoverability Reference

Patterns for making websites discoverable by AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude).

## llms.txt (Concise Summary)

Location: `public/llms.txt`

Follow the [llms.txt standard](https://llmstxt.org/). Structure:

```
# [Company Name]

> [One-line tagline/description]

[Company Name] is a [type] based in [location], specializing in [core services]. [Key differentiators].

## Services

- **[Service 1]**: [Brief description]
- **[Service 2]**: [Brief description]
- **[Service 3]**: [Brief description]

## [Products/SaaS] (if applicable)

- **[Product 1]**: [Brief description]. [Status: Pre-launch / Available]
- **[Product 2]**: [Brief description]. [Status]

## Pricing

- **[Tier 1]**: [Price] — [What's included]
- **[Tier 2]**: [Price] — [What's included]
- **[Tier 3]**: [Price] — [What's included]

## Key Facts

- Location: [City, Region, Country]
- Languages: [Languages]
- Founded: [Year]
- Website: [URL]
- Contact: [URL]

## Links

- [Full Company Profile](/llms-full.txt)
- [Website](/)
- [Services](/[locale]/services)
- [Contact](/[locale]/contact)
```

## How AI Chatbots Discover Businesses

1. **Web crawling** — GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot crawl public pages
2. **Structured data** — JSON-LD/Schema.org parsed for entity understanding
3. **Content relevance** — Clear, factual, well-structured content gets higher weight
4. **Authority signals** — Backlinks, citations, directory listings, reviews
5. **llms.txt** — Emerging standard for AI-optimized company summaries
6. **FAQ content** — Conversational Q&A matches AI query patterns
7. **E-E-A-T signals** — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

## Content Principles for AI Visibility

- **Answer questions directly**: Use H2/H3 as questions, answer in first paragraph, then elaborate
- **Be factual and specific**: Concrete numbers, real capabilities, measurable outcomes
- **Use natural language**: Write for humans; AI follows
- **Keep llms.txt current**: Update when services/pricing change
- **Build citations**: Directory listings, guest posts, mentions on authoritative sites
- **Structure content**: Clear headings, logical hierarchy, consistent formatting
